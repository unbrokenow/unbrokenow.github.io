The gap

Most budgeting apps run on needless subscriptions and shut crypto out

The subscription trap

  • Mint, YNAB, Copilot: five to fifteen dollars a month, forever
  • The budget you built gets held behind a paywall
  • Cancel the plan and your history often goes with it
  • A recurring bill just to keep reading numbers you typed in
  • A one time purchase should not need a subscription to work

No room for crypto

  • SOL, USDC and SKR holdings never touch a budget category
  • Staking rewards are invisible to every spending plan
  • No link between a wallet and a cost basis or tax record
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter support is basically nonexistent
  • A swap shows up nowhere in the monthly review
The idea

Every lockbox is a real Solana account, staked, lent, or locked by the network itself.

unBroke is paired with a noncustodial Solana wallet through the Mobile Wallet Adapter. Nothing you put into a lockbox sits in a database somewhere, it is delegated to a validator, supplied to a lending market, or staked with a guardian, and it comes back out the same way: a signed transaction, not a database update.

SKR integration

Staking SKR is a first class budgeting feature

01 · Stake

Delegate

Deposits build real stake transactions against the Guardian staking program and delegate straight to Solana Mobile's default Guardian, from inside a lockbox tied to a budget category.

02 · Earn

Realized APY

Yield is computed from each deposit's own recorded share price against the Guardian pool's current share price, so the figure reflects what that deposit actually earned, not an advertised number.

03 · Unstake

Unlock

Unlocking starts the Guardian's real 48 hour on chain cooldown, shown as a progress bar driven by actual chain state. Withdraw stays disabled until that cooldown has genuinely completed.

Mobile native

Mobile Wallet Adapter

Every deposit, unstake, withdraw, or penalty is a real signed Solana transaction. No private key ever touches the app.

Seed Vault encrypted backup

One wallet signature derives the backup's encryption key. Restoring on a new phone is the same signature, no account, no password, no server.

Biometric lock & weekly digest

Custody

What unBroke never does

No server holds your keys

unBroke never has your private key. Every transaction is signed on your device through the Mobile Wallet Adapter.

No custody of your funds

Lockboxes are positions on chain, under your own wallet. unBroke can show them to you, it cannot move them without your signature.

No hidden fees

Early exit penalties, when you choose a hard lock, are shown before you confirm and go to the validator or guardian, never to unBroke.
