unBroke

Track. Save. Thrive. Stay unBroke.

A zero-based budgeting app for Android. Give every unit of income a job, see where your money actually goes, and get a real date for when you'll be debt-free.

What it does

Zero-based budgeting

Every unit of income gets assigned to a category. No money left unaccounted for.

Savings with a goal

Set a monthly contribution and an overall target, then watch the progress bar fill.

Debt payoff projection

Enter balances, payments and interest. See exactly which month you'll be debt-free.

Monthly insights

Where your money went by category, month-by-month comparisons, and yearly totals.

Your data stays yours

  • Everything is stored on your own device. There is no account and no server.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • You can export a backup file at any time and keep it wherever you choose.
