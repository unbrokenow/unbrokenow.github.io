Privacy Policy

unBroke · Last updated 14 November 2026

← Back to unBroke

unBroke is a personal budgeting app that runs on your device. We have no accounts, no servers and no copy of your budget. The Vault is the one part that uses the internet, because it reads the Solana network.

Information we collect

None. unBroke has no user accounts, no sign in and no backend of our own. We never receive your budget, your wallet address or anything else you enter.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. That includes:

  • The name you choose during setup and your selected currency
  • Your income, budget categories and the amounts you assign to them
  • Expenses you log, including amounts, dates and any notes you add
  • Debts you track, including balances, monthly payments and interest rates
  • Savings contributions and targets
  • Your wallet's public address, if you connect one
  • Your lockboxes and any holdings you add by hand

This stays on your device unless you export it yourself. Uninstalling the app removes it.

The Vault and the Solana network

The Vault needs the internet to do its job. Here is exactly what that means.

When you connect a wallet, unBroke receives your public address and saves it on your device. We never see it, because we run no server of our own.

To show your balances and lockboxes, the app sends your public address to services that read the chain for you. Those are a public Solana node, the Solana Mobile staking service and Kamino. They can see your address and your IP address when the app calls them.

This is how every wallet app works. It is also worth knowing that the Solana blockchain is public and permanent. Anything you do on chain can be seen by anyone, forever.

Prices and exchange rates

To show what your assets are worth, unBroke fetches prices from CoinGecko and Jupiter, and currency rates from the Exchange Rate API.

Those services see a request for a price and your IP address. They do not receive your wallet address, your budget or your holdings.

Your keys and your signatures

unBroke never has your private key or your recovery phrase, and never asks for them. Every transaction is signed inside your own wallet app, which you approve yourself.

Backups you create

The backup feature writes a file of your budget data and hands it to your device's share menu. You decide where it goes, for example your own cloud storage or email.

Once you share that file, it falls under the privacy policy of whichever service you chose. We never receive a copy.

If you have a wallet connected, you can seal a backup so only that wallet can open it. The key is derived from one signature. There is no password and no account, and nothing is sent to us.

Demo mode

Demo mode uses made up data and no wallet. It makes no network calls and moves no real money.

Android system backup

unBroke permits Android Auto Backup. If it is on, Android may copy the app's data to your own Google Drive so it can be restored later.

That is a function of your device and your Google account, not of unBroke, and we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

unBroke contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. The app does not profile you and does not watch how you use it.

Permissions

The app asks for file access only when you save or restore a backup, and only for the file you pick. It uses the internet for the Vault features described above.

It does not ask for your contacts, location, camera, microphone, messages or bank accounts.

Third parties

We do not share, sell or rent your information, because we do not hold it. The services the app talks to are listed here, each with its own privacy policy:

  • A public Solana node, for reading balances and sending transactions you approve
  • Solana Mobile staking, for SKR lockboxes
  • Kamino, for USDC lockboxes
  • CoinGecko and Jupiter, for token prices
  • The Exchange Rate API, for currency conversion
  • Solscan, if you tap a link to view a transaction in your browser

App stores that distribute unBroke have their own policies covering download and installation.

Children

unBroke is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. The app collects no information from anyone, so no such data exists.

Your rights

Your data sits on your own device, so you control it directly. You can view, edit or delete any entry, export a full copy, and erase everything with "Reset and set up again" in Settings or by uninstalling the app.

There is no data held by us for you to request or have deleted. Activity already written to the Solana blockchain cannot be deleted by anyone, including us.

Changes to this policy

If unBroke adds a feature that sends data somewhere new, this policy will be updated before that feature ships.
