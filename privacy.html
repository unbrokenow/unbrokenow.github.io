Privacy Policy

unBroke · Last updated 17 August 2026

unBroke is a personal budgeting app that runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store or sell any of your personal or financial information.

Information we collect

None. unBroke has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no servers. We do not operate any backend that receives your data, and we have no ability to view what you enter into the app.

Information the app stores on your device

Everything you enter is saved locally in the app's private storage on your phone, including:

  • The name you choose during setup and your selected currency
  • Your income, budget categories and the amounts you assign to them
  • Expenses you log, including amounts, dates and any notes you add
  • Debts you track, including balances, monthly payments and interest rates
  • Savings contributions and targets

This information never leaves your device unless you choose to export it yourself. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

Backups you create

The app includes an optional backup feature. When you tap "Back up my data", unBroke writes a JSON file containing your budget information and hands it to your device's share menu. You decide where that file goes. For example your own cloud storage, email or messaging app. Once you share the file, it is governed by the privacy policy of whichever service you chose. We never receive a copy.

Android system backup

unBroke permits Android's built-in Auto Backup feature. If enabled on your device, Android may periodically copy the app's data to your own Google Drive account so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or set up a new phone. This is a function of your device and your Google account, not of unBroke, and we have no access to it. You can disable Android Auto Backup in your device settings.

Analytics, tracking and advertising

unBroke contains no analytics, no crash reporting services, no advertising SDKs and no tracking of any kind. The app does not build a profile of you and does not monitor how you use it.

Permissions

unBroke requests access to your file system only at the moment you choose to save or restore a backup, and only for the file you select. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, messages or bank accounts.

Third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or disclose your information to anyone, because we do not have it. The app is distributed through app stores which have their own privacy policies covering the download and installation process.

Children

unBroke is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. As the app collects no information from anyone, no such data exists.

Your rights

Because your data is held only on your own device, you have direct and complete control of it. You can view, edit or delete any entry inside the app, export a full copy at any time, and erase everything using "Reset and set up again" in Settings or by uninstalling the app. There is no data held by us for you to request access to or deletion of.

Changes to this policy

If unBroke ever adds features that involve transmitting data, for example; optional cloud sync, this policy will be updated before those features are released, and the change will be described in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to mybrokeman@gmail.com.
