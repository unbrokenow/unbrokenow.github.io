How lockboxes work

Three assets, three real positions

SOL

Validator staking

SOL delegates to a validator under a hard lock or a cooldown lock you choose when the lockbox is created. Rewards accrue the same way they would in any Solana wallet, just tied to a budget category.

USDC

Kamino lending

USDC is supplied to Kamino Lend for real, on chain yield. Your lockbox balance reflects the lending position itself, not an internal ledger entry.

SKR

Guardian staking

SKR stakes with the Solana Mobile Guardian. Deposits build real stake transactions and delegate straight to Solana Mobile's default Guardian from inside a lockbox.

SKR staking in detail

What actually happens when you lock SKR

01 · Stake

Delegate

Deposits build real stake transactions against the Guardian staking program and delegate straight to Solana Mobile's default Guardian, from inside a lockbox tied to a budget category.

02 · Earn

Realized APY

Yield is computed from each deposit's own recorded share price against the Guardian pool's current share price, so the figure reflects what that deposit actually earned, not an advertised number.

03 · Unstake

Unlock

Unlocking starts the Guardian's real 48 hour on chain cooldown, shown as a progress bar driven by actual chain state. Withdraw stays disabled until that cooldown has genuinely completed.

Net worth

Custody

What unBroke never does

No server holds your keys

unBroke never has your private key. Every transaction is signed on your device through the Mobile Wallet Adapter.

No custody of your funds

Lockboxes are positions on chain, under your own wallet. unBroke can show them to you, it cannot move them without your signature.

No hidden fees

Early exit penalties, when you choose a hard lock, are shown before you confirm and go to the validator or guardian, never to unBroke.

FAQ

Common questions

Does the Vault ever take custody of my funds?

No. Lockboxes are real positions under your own wallet. unBroke reads them from the chain and can build the transactions, but every signature happens on your device through the Mobile Wallet Adapter.

What if I hold crypto on an exchange or another wallet?

Add it manually from the Net worth screen. Search for the asset, enter what you hold, and it sits alongside your on chain Wallet and Locked balances in the same total, priced in real time.

How is profit and loss calculated?

unBroke compares a holding's current live price against what you paid for it, whether that holding came from your connected wallet, a lockbox, or one you added by hand.
