The Vault, and the risks that come with it

A lockbox is a real position on the Solana network, not a savings account. Please read this section before you lock anything.

  • The value of what you lock can fall, including to nothing. You can get back less than you put in.
  • Yield is never guaranteed. Rates change, and past returns say nothing about future ones.
  • A SOL hard lock is enforced by Solana's own stake program. Once it is set, nobody can unlock it early. That includes you, and it includes us.
  • Cooldown periods are set by the network, not by us. We cannot speed one up, cancel one or make an exception.
  • Penalties you choose for early unlocking or a missed goal go to the validator or guardian. unBroke never receives them.
  • Lockboxes rely on other projects, such as Kamino and Solana Mobile staking. Those carry their own risks, including bugs, outages and failure. We do not control them.
  • Transactions on Solana are final. A transfer sent to the wrong place cannot be reversed.

Only lock money you can afford to leave alone, and only lock what you can afford to lose.
