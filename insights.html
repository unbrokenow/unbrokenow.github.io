FAQ

Common questions

Do Everyday and Vault stats live in the same view?

Yes. Stats and Insights pulls from both sides of the app, so your fiat budget and your Solana positions show up together instead of in two disconnected tabs.

Where do profit and loss figures come from?

From the signed transactions themselves. Every stake, unstake, lend, or delegate is a real Solana transaction, so the gain or loss on each one is calculated from what actually happened on chain, not an estimate.

Can I get this data out of the app?

Yes. Export your full history as CSV or Excel at any time from the Stats screen, no subscription or server round trip required.
